Desktop questions
What happens after checkout?

You are sent to the access page, where the purchase email can be used to load the licence record and unlock the installer when the webhook has completed.

What if the licence is not visible immediately?

Paddle webhook processing can complete shortly after checkout. Use “Check subscription” again on the access page. The purchase email must match the Paddle transaction.

Can I move a licence to another device?

Yes. Open the account page, load the licence and release an old device seat before activating the replacement device.

Does this replace approved manuals?

No. It is a study-support tool only and is not an approved AFM, QRH, MEL, checklist, company manual or operational authority.
