Account and Data Deletion

Request deletion from the Android app or by sending a verified request from the email address linked to the account.

App and service: DHC-6 TrainerDeveloper: TJ AeronauticalLast updated: August 5, 2026Contact: tj.aeronautical@outlook.com

Request Deletion In The App

  1. Open DHC-6 Trainer on your Android device.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Tap Request Account/Data Deletion.
  4. Follow the email prompt so the account can be identified and the request confirmed.

Request Deletion By Email

Email tj.aeronautical@outlook.com with the subject DHC-6 Trainer Account Deletion Request. Send the request from the email used in the app where possible and state whether you want account deletion, training-data deletion or both.

Create deletion email

What Will Be Deleted

  • Applicable Firebase account identifiers for the DHC-6 Trainer account.
  • Applicable cloud-synced profile, training progress, quiz results, settings and organisation links associated with the account.
  • Support records directly tied to the request after they are no longer needed to complete or document it.
  • Desktop licence and activation data where deletion is requested and retention is not required for billing, security, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

What May Be Retained

  • Google Play or Paddle purchase, refund, tax, fraud-prevention, accounting or legal records where retention is required.
  • Aggregated or de-identified analytics that no longer identify the account.
  • Records required to complete the deletion request, prevent abuse or establish that the request was fulfilled.

Browser Trainer Data

Live web-trainer scores and logbook history are stored locally in the browser. You can remove them using the trainer's clear-logbook control or by clearing site data in the browser.

Subscriptions

Deleting an account does not automatically cancel a Google Play or Paddle subscription. Cancel Android subscriptions through Google Play and desktop subscriptions through the Paddle billing portal before or after submitting the deletion request.

Verification and Timing

We may ask you to verify ownership of the account. We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days unless a longer period is required for security, fraud prevention, billing or legal reasons.

If you cannot access the app, the email method above is sufficient to start the request. Never include passwords or payment-card details.
