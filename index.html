Frequently asked

Know exactly what the trainer is—and is not.

DHC-6 Trainer is designed to support structured study. It never replaces approved operational documentation or company procedures.

Is this an approved operational reference?

No. DHC-6 Trainer is a study and training-support product. Always use the current approved AFM, QRH, MEL, company manuals, checklists and regulatory documents for aircraft operation.

Can subscribers use the trainer without Google Play?

Yes. Open the Subscriber Web App and confirm your active subscription using your purchase email and licence key.

Where is browser training data stored?

The live web trainer stores session scores and logbook data locally in your browser. Clearing site data removes that local history.

How does desktop access work?

Subscribe through secure Paddle checkout, recover your licence on the access page, download the Windows installer and activate the app with your licence key.
