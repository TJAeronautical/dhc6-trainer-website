Recovery help
My purchase completed but no licence appears.

Confirm that the email exactly matches the Paddle checkout email, then check again. Subscription webhooks can finish after the browser reaches this page.

I lost the licence key.

Load the subscription with the purchase email. The account summary can recover the linked key when the subscription record is available.

The download buttons remain disabled.

The licence must be active and the desktop release source must be configured on the production service. Contact support with the purchase email if the status is active but no installer can be prepared.

How do I cancel or update payment details?

Load the subscription, then open the Paddle billing portal for invoices, payment-method updates, renewal and cancellation options.

Need account support?

Send the purchase email from the affected subscription.

Do not send payment-card details or passwords. Include the licence key only when available.
