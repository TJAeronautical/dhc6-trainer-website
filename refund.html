Refund and Cancellation Policy

How trials, subscription cancellations, refund requests and statutory consumer rights apply to DHC-6 Trainer purchases.

Service: DHC-6 TrainerProvider: TJ AeronauticalEffective: August 5, 2026Contact: tj.aeronautical@outlook.com

This policy applies to purchases of DHC-6 Trainer subscriptions, digital access and related software. The payment provider shown at checkout processes the transaction and may act as merchant of record. This policy supplements the provider’s buyer terms and does not limit rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

1. Desktop Purchases Through Paddle

Paddle is the merchant of record and authorised reseller for applicable DHC-6 Trainer desktop subscriptions. Paddle handles payment collection, invoicing, tax and approved refunds. Refund requests for Paddle purchases may be submitted through Paddle’s buyer support channels or by contacting us at tj.aeronautical@outlook.com with the purchase email and transaction details. We may assist with investigation and submit or support a refund request, but Paddle processes the final payment reversal.

2. Android Purchases Through Google Play

Android purchases and subscriptions made through Google Play are subject to Google Play’s billing, cancellation and refund procedures. Refund requests should ordinarily be submitted through Google Play using the Google account that made the purchase. We can assist with entitlement issues but cannot directly reverse every Google Play transaction.

3. Free Trials

  • Where a free trial is offered, its duration is displayed before checkout.
  • You will not be charged during the free-trial period unless the checkout terms expressly state otherwise.
  • The subscription automatically converts to a paid plan at the end of the trial unless cancelled before the trial expires.
  • Cancel through the Paddle customer portal or the applicable platform subscription settings before the stated renewal time to avoid the first charge.

A trial is intended to allow evaluation of compatibility and suitability. We recommend testing the web trainer, reviewing system requirements and cancelling before trial expiry if the product does not meet your needs.

4. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel a subscription at any time through the applicable billing portal. Cancellation normally prevents future renewal and leaves access active until the end of the already-paid billing period. Cancellation does not automatically refund a completed charge unless a refund is required by law or approved under this policy or the payment provider’s terms.

Deleting a DHC-6 Trainer account or uninstalling the application does not cancel a subscription. Cancellation must be completed through Paddle, Google Play or the billing route used for purchase.

5. When Refunds May Be Approved

Subject to the payment provider’s procedures and mandatory law, a full or partial refund may be considered where:

  • you were charged after cancelling before the applicable renewal deadline;
  • a duplicate or clearly erroneous charge occurred;
  • the purchased service was materially unavailable or defective and we could not provide a reasonable remedy;
  • the purchase was unauthorised, subject to identity and fraud checks;
  • the product description or plan delivered was materially different from what was purchased;
  • a statutory cooling-off, withdrawal, quality or consumer-remedy right applies; or
  • exceptional circumstances justify a discretionary refund.

6. Requests That Are Normally Not Refundable

Except where mandatory consumer law provides otherwise, refunds are not normally granted solely because:

  • you changed your mind after substantial use of the digital service;
  • you forgot to cancel before a renewal after clear renewal disclosure;
  • you no longer need the product, changed employment or changed training plans;
  • your device does not meet published requirements and the issue is not caused by the Service;
  • you expected the Service to replace approved manuals, company procedures, an instructor or operational training;
  • access was restricted because of fraud, chargeback abuse, unauthorised licence sharing or material breach of the Terms; or
  • a feature was used incorrectly or without consulting available support and documentation.

7. How to Request a Refund

Email tj.aeronautical@outlook.com with:

  • the purchase email address;
  • the Paddle transaction or subscription ID, Google Play order number, or receipt;
  • the purchase date and plan;
  • a concise explanation of the problem and steps already taken; and
  • any relevant screenshots, without payment-card details or passwords.

Submit requests promptly. We aim to acknowledge support requests within five business days, although investigation and payment-provider processing may take longer. Approval timing and the time for funds to appear depend on the payment provider, payment method and financial institution.

8. Effect of a Refund or Chargeback

When a full refund, reversal or chargeback is completed, the associated subscription, licence, protected download access and device activations may be terminated or disabled. A partial refund may result in a corresponding plan, term or entitlement adjustment. Local training records may remain on your device or browser until you remove them.

9. Service Defects and Support Before Refund

Where practical, we may first offer troubleshooting, account recovery, a corrected installer, licence repair, access restoration, replacement access or another reasonable remedy. Accepting technical assistance does not waive mandatory consumer rights.

10. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits statutory rights that apply to you. Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning digital-content conformity, cooling-off periods, misleading representations, defective services or unauthorised transactions. Where a mandatory rule conflicts with this policy, the mandatory rule prevails.

For South African consumers, applicable rights may arise under the Consumer Protection Act and Electronic Communications and Transactions Act. International customers may also have non-waivable rights under the laws of their country of residence.

11. Fraud and Abuse

We and our payment providers may refuse, delay or reverse a refund where there is credible evidence of fraud, account takeover, abusive repeated refund requests, material licence misuse or false information. This does not affect legitimate disputes or statutory remedies.

12. Policy Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in products, payment providers or law. The current version and effective date will be published on this page. Changes will not retroactively remove rights already accrued under applicable law.

13. Contact

For billing, cancellation or refund assistance, contact tj.aeronautical@outlook.com. Include the purchase email and transaction reference, but never send full payment-card details.

DHC-6 Trainer is a training-support product only. It is not an approved operational reference and does not replace approved manuals, company procedures, instructor guidance or aviation regulations.
