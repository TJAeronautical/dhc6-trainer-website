Privacy and data use

Privacy Policy

How DHC-6 Trainer and its related services collect, use, protect and delete account, training, purchase and diagnostic information.

App and service: DHC-6 TrainerDeveloper: TJ AeronauticalLast updated: August 5, 2026Contact: tj.aeronautical@outlook.com

DHC-6 Trainer is an aviation training and study application. This policy explains what information the Android app, desktop licensing service, website and related services may collect, why it is used, and how you can request deletion.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as email address and authentication identifiers when you create or sign in to an account.
  • Training progress, settings, quiz results, pack access, organisation links and app preferences used to provide the learning experience.
  • Purchase and entitlement information from Google Play Billing or Paddle so paid content, desktop subscriptions and licence access can be verified and restored.
  • Desktop licence information such as licence status, plan, activation identifiers, device names and activation timestamps.
  • Diagnostic information such as crash reports, performance signals, device type, app version and basic usage events through relevant platform services.
  • Text you enter into optional AI-powered training features, such as the Oral Examiner, when you choose to use those features.

How We Use Information

  • To sign you in, sync progress, restore purchases and provide access to training content.
  • To issue, validate and manage desktop licences and protected installer downloads.
  • To maintain reliability, investigate crashes and improve the training experience.
  • To process optional AI training prompts and generate training-support responses.
  • To respond to support, account, purchase and data-deletion requests.

Service Providers

The service may use Google Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Crashlytics, Analytics, App Check, Google Play Billing, Paddle, Cloudflare and OpenAI-powered services. These providers process information as needed to operate the features described above and under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

AI Training Features

AI features are optional learning aids. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, medical information, confidential company material or identifiable operational flight details. AI responses can be wrong and must not replace official manuals, instructor guidance, approved procedures or regulatory requirements.

Payments and Subscriptions

Google Play processes applicable Android purchases. Paddle acts as merchant of record for applicable desktop subscriptions and handles payment, invoicing and tax. DHC-6 Trainer receives the identifiers and entitlement information required to confirm access; it does not require you to send payment-card details directly to TJ Aeronautical.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the service, comply with law, prevent fraud, process purchases or respond to user requests.

Retention

Account, progress, entitlement and licence records are kept while the relevant account or access remains active and for a reasonable period needed for recovery, security, fraud prevention, accounting or legal obligations. Diagnostic data follows the retention settings of the relevant provider.

Your Choices

  • Use guest, local or offline features where available.
  • Request account and associated data deletion through the Account and Data Deletion page.
  • Manage Android permissions through device settings.
  • Cancel Android subscriptions through Google Play and desktop subscriptions through the Paddle billing portal. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel an active subscription.
  • Clear browser data to remove locally stored web-trainer scores and logbook history.

Children

DHC-6 Trainer is intended for aviation learners and professionals and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

Security

We use platform security features and service-provider protections intended to protect information. No transmission or storage method is perfectly secure; contact us if you believe an account or licence has been compromised.

Changes

This policy may be updated as product features, service providers or legal requirements change. The current version and update date will remain published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, support or deletion requests, email tj.aeronautical@outlook.com.

DHC-6 Trainer is for training support only. Always use current official manuals, approved procedures, instructor guidance and applicable aviation regulations.
